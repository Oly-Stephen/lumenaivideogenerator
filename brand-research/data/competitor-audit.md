**Method.** Each public homepage was opened in a browser on 2026-10-13. The computed styles were read with JavaScript: the H1 font, the body font, the page background, the `theme-color` meta tag and the visible call-to-action buttons.

**Rules followed.** No sign-in on any site, and no cookie banners accepted. Values are what the live page rendered at that moment. Sites A/B test and change often, so treat these as a dated snapshot.
